Cooperative Climate Action in Business & Industry

Businesses influence energy use, transport and materials. Their climate action becomes stronger when companies cooperate instead of trying to solve every problem alone. This page explains methods that cooperation can support cleaner industry.

Renewable Energy Transitions in Communities

Renewable Energy Transitions in Communities represents the collective shift toward sustainable, decentralized power generation. This local strategy minimizes fossil fuel reliance, making it a powerful vehicle for achieving the ultimate climate action objective.

Climate Communication

Climate communication helps people understand climate change, its impacts and the actions that can be taken to protect the environment. Clear and reliable communication can connect scientific information with everyday life.

Global Technology & Knowledge Sharing

No single country holds every answer to climate change. Progress moves faster when nations, researchers and communities share what works. This page explains four ways that sharing technology and knowledge can strengthen climate action around the world.
